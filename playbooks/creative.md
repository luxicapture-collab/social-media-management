# Creative playbook

The job is not "make posts". The job is to make the one piece of content this audience would
stop for, remember, and act on — and to prove it with numbers. Generic is failure.

## Principles

1. **The goal decides the mix; the scenario decides the story.** Read the goal and the funnel
   diagnosis before you choose formats. Read the scenario before you choose the story.
2. **One idea per piece.** If you need "and" to describe it, it is two pieces.
3. **Specific beats clever.** A real number, a real place, a real person, a real object.
   "We restored a 1962 wedding film frame by frame" beats "We bring memories to life".
4. **Show the change.** Most great social content is a visible change: before → after,
   problem → resolution, raw → finished, question → answer, expectation → reality.
5. **Earn the first second.** The first frame must be a pattern interrupt that is still
   on-brand: motion, contrast, a face, an unexpected object, a question on screen.
6. **Native, not repurposed.** Write and cut for each platform. A LinkedIn post is not an
   Instagram caption with longer paragraphs.
7. **Taste over trends.** Use a trend only when it serves the brand's voice and the goal.

## Angle library (generate at least five before choosing)

| Angle | What it is | Best for |
|---|---|---|
| transformation | before → after, raw → graded, empty → finished | reach, saves |
| behind_the_scenes | the process nobody sees: setup, mistakes, craft | trust, followers |
| teach_one_thing | one useful insight in < 30 s, or a 5–8 frame carousel | saves, authority, LinkedIn |
| pov_story | first-person moment ("POV: the bride sees the film for the first time") | emotion, shares |
| social_proof | client reaction, testimonial, result with numbers | conversion |
| myth_vs_reality | "Everyone thinks X. Here's what actually happens." | comments, reach |
| trend_remix | a current format bent to the brand's own story | discovery |
| surreal_hook | one impossible-but-photoreal image that breaks one law (see below) | stopping power |
| founder_voice | the founder's opinion, said plainly, with a reason | LinkedIn, X, loyalty |
| challenge | an invitation the audience can answer (story polls, "guess the year") | engagement, stories |

Score each angle 1–10 on: **goal fit**, **novelty for this audience** (have they seen this from
us recently?), **production cost**, **platform fit**. Keep the best two to four.

### Surreal hook discipline
One broken law per frame, obeyed ruthlessly, delivered through ordinary photographic physics.
The world stays photoreal; only one thing is impossible (a wedding veil that becomes fog over
the city; a camera lens that reflects a different decade). Never stack surreal effects.

## Hooks (first 1–2 seconds)

Pair a visual hook with a verbal/on-screen hook. Formulas that work:

- **Cold open on the climax**: start at the most emotional frame, then rewind.
- **The specific number**: "312 hours of footage. One 90-second film."
- **The contradiction**: "Our most expensive shot cost $0."
- **The question they already have**: "Why does wedding footage look cheap? Lighting."
- **The reveal setup**: "Watch the left side of the frame."
- **POV**: "POV: you hear your grandmother's voice for the first time in 20 years."
- **The mistake**: "We ruined this shot. Here's how we saved it."

On-screen hook text: 3–7 words, high contrast, inside the safe area (not in the bottom 20 %
or top 12 % where the UI sits).

## Structures

- **Reel (15–35 s)**: hook (0–2 s) → tension/build (2–10 s) → payoff (10–25 s) → loop or CTA.
  Cut every 1.5–3 s unless a long take is the point. End on a frame that loops into the first.
- **Story set (3–6 frames)**: context → moment → interaction (poll / question / quiz / slider)
  → link or DM prompt. ≤ 10 words per frame. One sticker per frame.
- **Carousel (5–10 frames)**: frame 1 = hook promise; frames 2–N–1 = one point each, big type;
  last frame = summary + save/share prompt. Visual continuity across swipes.
- **LinkedIn**: insight in the first two lines (≤ 210 characters), then the story or proof in
  short paragraphs, then a question that invites expert answers. Native video or a document
  carousel (PDF) outperforms link posts.
- **X**: one sharp idea, opinion or number. Short video or one strong image. No threads.

Copy frameworks to reach for: PAS (problem, agitate, solve), AIDA, before–after–bridge,
open loop (promise early, pay off late), and "1 idea, 1 example, 1 action".

## House look (unless the client's visual grammar says otherwise)

Photoreal cinema. ARRI Alexa 65 sensor look, Kodak Vision3 500T grain and halation, anamorphic
lenses (oval bokeh, gentle horizontal flares), motivated chiaroscuro lighting, practical lights
in frame, shallow depth of field, natural skin texture. Colour: warm highlights, deep but not
crushed shadows. **Never** CGI, plastic skin, HDR look, oversaturation, illustration or cartoon.

### Prompt grammar for generated shots
Write every shot prompt as: **subject + action → setting + time of day → lens + framing →
light → camera move → grade/film stock**. Example:

> A bride's hands unfold a yellowed letter, fingers trembling slightly; candle-lit vintage room
> at dusk; 50mm anamorphic close-up, shallow depth of field; warm practical candle key light,
> soft window fill; slow push-in; Kodak Vision3 500T, gentle halation, fine grain.

Keep one subject's appearance identical across shots (same wardrobe words, same hair, same
age). Generate a keyframe still first and animate it (image-to-video) when continuity matters.

## Sound

- Voiceover: conversational, 2.4–2.8 words per second in English (≈ 2.2 in Farsi). Write for
  the ear: short sentences, concrete nouns, no lists of adjectives.
- Music: name the emotion and the instrumentation ("intimate solo piano, sparse, rising
  strings at the payoff, 80 bpm"), never a copyrighted artist.
- Sound design sells realism: room tone, film-projector clatter, page turns, breath.

## Text on screen

Burned-in captions for every voiced reel (most people watch muted). 2–4 words per caption
chunk, highlight the key word. Respect safe zones. Persian text: right-to-left, never mixed
with Latin in the same line unless it is a brand name.

## Anti-patterns (instant rewrite)

"Elevate", "unlock", "unleash", "game-changer", "in today's fast-paced world", "look no
further", "dive into", "take it to the next level", "we're thrilled to announce", rhetorical
questions with obvious answers, emoji walls, stock-photo scenarios, generic drone shots with
inspirational music, hashtag stuffing.
