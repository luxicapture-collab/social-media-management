# Platform rules (kept current by the daily watch)

Last reviewed: 2026-10. The daily watch proposes edits to this file when platforms change;
approved proposals are appended below the "Updates" heading.

## Instagram (professional accounts via the Meta Graph API)

- **Formats**: Reels (9:16, 1080×1920), Stories (9:16), feed image/video (4:5, 1080×1350),
  carousels (2–10 items, 4:5). Trial Reels show to non-followers first — use them to test a
  risky hook; graduation (sharing to followers) is MANUAL in the app or automatic on
  performance (`SS_PERFORMANCE`).
- **Captions**: ≤ 2,200 characters; the first ~125 characters show before "more" — the hook
  must work alone. Hashtags: **3–5 specific tags, never more than 5** (Instagram caps posts at
  five). Links in captions are not clickable → use "link in bio" or a story link sticker.
- **What the ranking rewards (2026)**: sends/shares per reach, watch time and completion, saves,
  and original content. Reposted watermarked content is down-ranked.
- **AI disclosure**: label AI-generated or AI-altered media as such.
- **API facts**: 100 API-published posts per 24 h per account; publishing needs a public HTTPS
  media URL; stickers (polls, links, questions) cannot be added via the API — those stories are
  sent to the phone to finish by hand.
- **Insights**: views, reach, likes, comments, shares, saved, reposts, total_interactions,
  profile_visits, follows (feed), ig_reels_avg_watch_time (reels), story navigation. Audience
  demographics (age, gender, city, country) need ≥ 100 followers.

## Facebook Pages

- Reels (9:16, 3–90 s), Stories, photo/video/album posts, link posts.
- Captions: conversational, a little longer than Instagram; links are fine; 0–2 hashtags.
- Comments and shares drive distribution; reply to every comment in the first hour.

## LinkedIn (Community Management API, versioned REST)

- Organisation pages and personal profiles. Native video (≤ 15 min recommended; 4:5 or 1:1),
  images, multi-image, **document carousels (PDF)** — the strongest organic format for saves.
- Commentary ≤ 3,000 characters; ~210 characters show before "see more": lead with the business
  insight. ≤ 3 hashtags. **Outbound links go in the first comment**, not the body.
- What works: specific lessons from real work, numbers, frameworks, contrarian-but-reasoned
  takes, questions that invite expert replies. Dwell time and meaningful comments drive reach.
- No DM API for most apps; comments can be read and answered on organisation posts.
- Analytics: impressions, unique impressions, clicks, reactions, comments, reposts, engagement;
  follower demographics are professional (seniority, industry, function, region) — not age.

## X

- One sharp idea per post, ≤ 280 weighted characters (URLs count 23, emoji 2) unless the account
  has Premium. No threads from the agent.
- **API is pay-per-use**: ~$0.015 per post, **$0.20 per post containing a link**, $0.005 per
  post read, $0.01 per reply. Avoid links; batch reads; budget replies.
- Short native video (≤ 2:20) or one strong image. 0–1 hashtag.
- Metrics: impressions, likes, replies, reposts, quotes, bookmarks, URL clicks, profile clicks
  (non-public metrics only for the account's own posts in the last 30 days). No demographics.

## Cross-platform cadence defaults (per client, adjustable)

Instagram: 3 reels + 2 feed posts + daily stories per week. LinkedIn: 2–3 posts per week.
X: 3–5 posts per week. Facebook: mirror the strongest Instagram pieces with native captions.
Never two feed posts within 4 hours on the same account; at most one reel per day.
