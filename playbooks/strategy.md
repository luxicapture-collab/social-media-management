# Strategy and pivot playbook

The agent is accountable to the goal Morteza sets for each client. Content that looks good but
doesn't move the KPI gets replaced. Pivot as often as the data says — but change one lever at a
time so we learn what worked.

## Reading the numbers honestly

- Never invent metrics. If a platform didn't report something, say "not reported".
- Judge a post at 24 h, 72 h and 7 days; most of its life is in the first 72 h.
- Compare with the client's own median (last 30–60 days), not with other accounts, once there
  are 8+ posts. Before that, use broad benchmarks and say so.
- Engagement rate = (likes + comments + shares + saves) ÷ reach × 100.
- A goal is **behind pace** when actual progress < 90 % of the straight-line path to the target;
  **off track** below 70 %. Two behind-pace daily checks in a row trigger a pivot review.

## Find the weakest link before changing anything

| Symptom | Bottleneck | Levers |
|---|---|---|
| Low reach, normal engagement rate | **Distribution** | posting time, Trial Reels, format mix (more reels), collaborations/collab posts, trending audio, shareable topics |
| Reach fine, early drop-off / low 3-s retention | **Hook** | first frame, first line, on-screen text, cold open, faster first cut |
| Watched, few saves/shares | **Resonance** | topic pillar, specificity, utility (teach one thing), emotion, carousel how-tos |
| Engagement fine, few profile visits/clicks/leads | **Conversion** | CTA wording, story follow-ups with link stickers, bio/link placement, offer clarity, DM prompts |

Weight the bottleneck by the KPI: a followers goal cares most about distribution and
conversion; an engagement goal about resonance; a views goal about hook and distribution.

## Experiments

- **One major lever per client per week.** Micro-adjustments (time slot, CTA wording,
  hashtag set) are allowed daily and don't count as the major lever.
- Each experiment: finding → change → expected effect (with a number) → metric → review date
  (≥ 3 posts or 7 days). Tag affected content with the experiment id.
- On the review date: compare tagged posts with the baseline median. Keep (lift ≥ +15 %),
  revert (≤ −10 %), or inconclusive (extend once). Record the learning as an insight.
- Insights are durable memory. Re-read them before every plan; retire ones the data contradicts.

## Format mix by goal

| Goal | Lead with | Support with |
|---|---|---|
| Followers / reach | Reels (Trial Reels for risky hooks), collab posts | stories for retention |
| Engagement | carousels, myth-vs-reality reels, polls in stories | comment prompts |
| Leads / clicks | stories with link stickers, LinkedIn documents, social proof | DM prompts |
| Authority (B2B) | LinkedIn documents and native video, founder voice | X opinions |

## When the goal is far off

1. Say it plainly in the goal read: where we are, what's failing, what we're changing.
2. Re-plan the next week's unproduced concepts around the pivot (revise, don't just add more).
3. If two pivots in a row fail, question the goal's assumptions (audience, offer, platform) and
   flag it for Morteza.
